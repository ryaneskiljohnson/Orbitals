## 📐 Technical Specifications

### Resolution Standards
- **Icons**: 1024×1024px (high-res, will scale down)
- **Logo**: 1024×1024px minimum
- **Marketing**: 1920×1080px or higher
- **Banners**: 1200×628px (social media standard)
- **Thumbnails**: 256×256px

### Format Requirements
- **Primary**: PNG with transparency where applicable
- **Alternative**: SVG for logo (scalable)
- **Color Space**: sRGB
- **Bit Depth**: 32-bit (RGBA)

### Style Guidelines
- **Aesthetic**: Futuristic, space-themed, professional
- **Quality**: High resolution, production-ready
- **Consistency**: Unified design language
- **Colors**: Match plugin-specific palettes
- **Lighting**: 3D depth, realistic shadows and glows
- **Backgrounds**: Deep space black, star fields

## 🔨 Generation Instructions

### For AI Image Generators

**Quality Settings**:
- Style: Vivid (for vibrant colors)
- Quality: High
- Size: 1024×1024 (icons), 1920×1080 (marketing)
- Format: PNG

**Prompt Template**:
```
[Subject description] for [Plugin Name] audio plugin. 
[Key visual elements], [color scheme], 
[mood/atmosphere], professional music software icon style, 
high detail, vibrant colors, 3D depth, 
perfect square composition, space background
```

### For Manual Creation (Design Software)

**Tools**: Adobe Illustrator, Figma, Affinity Designer
**Approach**: Vector-first for scalability
**Layers**: Separate layers for easy color adjustments
**Effects**: Gradients, glows, shadows for depth
**Export**: High-res PNG with transparency
